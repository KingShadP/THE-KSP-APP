# KingShadP Internal AI Instructions

When making any changes to this application, adhere strictly to the following brand guidelines:

## Core Identity
**KingShadP** is a music artist persona and luxury creative universe built around power, identity, confidence, and self-mythology. The brand is a Ruler-led luxury creative empire with Creator, Magician, and Outlaw energy. 

## The Brand Aesthetic
- **Colors**: Matte Black (`#050505`), Oxblood Crimson (`#5E0008`), Rose Gold / Copper (`#B76E79`), Ivory / Off-White, Platinum.
- **Motifs**: Dragon/Giragon, SP Crest, Crown, Portal, Chalice, Black Marble, Gold Veining, Crimson Velvet.
- **Tone & Voice**: Commanding, refined, cinematic, unbothered, expensive, visionary, dangerous when necessary. Never loud, desperate, or generic streetwear. Use terms like "standard", "presence", "legacy", "archive", "vault", "relic", "portal", "command". Avoid overusing "royal", "boss", "drip", "hustle".

## Concept
The central duality is **The Creator // The Create**. The brand sells presence, taste, ambition, mythology, and identity—not just merchandise or clothing. Every product is a "wearable artifact".

## Frontend Guidelines
- Rely on high-end luxury styling.
- Minimize UI clutter. Let negative space highlight the brand elements.
- Digital effects (like glowing elements, neon, or glitch) should be treated as subtle "seasoning" layered underneath a primarily tactile and refined physical luxury foundation.
